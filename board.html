Revenue, gross and net come from the QuickBooks P&L by Class. Field payroll (wages, EI, CPP) is booked in QuickBooks as an expense; until the bookkeepers move it, the dashboard moves each department's field payroll into cost of sales so gross margins include direct labour, and moves owner dividends to Administration. Company net is unchanged by that move. Revenue matches the QuickBooks P&L exactly (Jun-Sep 263,053). Progress invoicing is off, so each Housecall Pro invoice reaches QuickBooks at its own amount and month. The only other change is a 2,000 dividend dated Aug 31 in QuickBooks that was sent Sep 1 (counted in September). Department net = the department's own profit after direct labour, minus its share of overhead (Administration and unclassed costs), shared by each department's revenue; the three departments add up to company net. Ticket, jobs and close ratio come from Housecall Pro; a rebate claim is folded into the homeowner's job, so one consultation is one ticket.
